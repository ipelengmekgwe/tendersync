# ADR-0015: No automated bid submission

**Status:** Accepted (23 Oct 2026)

## Context

Plan.txt instructs an evaluation first. Online submission on eTenders needs a registered supplier login; automating it carries terms-of-use, account and deadline risk, and the publisher says the data is not for critical decisions.

## Decision

The system never submits a bid. It stops at a human-approved, submission-ready package with explicit submission instructions.

## Consequences

Re-evaluate only with written confirmation from National Treasury and after Phases 2 and 3 are stable.
